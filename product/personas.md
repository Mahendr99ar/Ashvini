# Personas

These are working assumptions written before any interviews. Each one ends with the questions we still need real users to answer. Update this file after every conversation and note who it was with (role only, never a name without permission).

## Priya, flight dynamics engineer at a small Earth observation startup

- Two satellites in sun-synchronous orbit at about 520 km. A team of three runs operations.
- Gets conjunction warnings by email from Space-Track. Opens each one, checks miss distance and Pc, and asks a senior colleague when in doubt.
- **The decision she owns:** manoeuvre, monitor or dismiss, usually 24 to 72 hours before closest approach.
- **What hurts:** too many warnings, no history per event, no idea whether the Pc will go up or down as tracking improves, and a storm can make yesterday's numbers wrong.
- **What she needs from Ashvini:** a ranked list, a prediction of where the risk is heading with an uncertainty band, and an alert in Slack only when it matters.
- **Open questions:** how many warnings a week, really? What Pc threshold does her team use, and who chose it? Would she upload her own ephemeris?

## Daniel, space-safety analyst at an agency or research group

- Watches the whole environment rather than one fleet. Writes reports after break-ups, storms and close calls.
- **The decision he owns:** what to flag to leadership and the public, and how confidently.
- **What hurts:** stitching together catalogues, space-weather data and news by hand.
- **What he needs:** replays of past events, debris families after a break-up, exports he can cite.
- **Open questions:** which exports matter (CSV, CCSDS, images)? How does he cite data today?

## Meera, science journalist

- Covers launches, debris and space weather for a general audience.
- **The decision she owns:** whether a story is real, and how to explain it without exaggerating.
- **What she needs:** a clear view of what is overhead, plain-language explanations, and sources she can link.
- **Open questions:** what makes her trust a tool she has never heard of?
