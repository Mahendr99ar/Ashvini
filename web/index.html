    References

    Ashvini builds on published research and open data from space agencies, operators and universities. Every model is trained on one of these datasets and compared with a plain baseline.

    2. Pinto, F. et al. (2020). Towards automated satellite conjunction management with Bayesian deep learning. arXiv:2012.12450
    3. European Space Agency. Collision Avoidance Challenge dataset. Zenodo 4463683
    4. Foster, J. L. & Estes, H. S. (1992). A parametric analysis of orbital debris collision probability and maneuver rate for space vehicles. NASA JSC-25898.
    5. Alfano, S. (2005). Relating position uncertainty to maximum conjunction probability. Journal of the Astronautical Sciences, 53(2).
    6. Hoots, F. R., Crawford, L. L. & Roehrich, R. L. (1984). An analytic method to determine future close approaches between satellites. Celestial Mechanics, 33.
    7. Flohrer, T. et al. (2008). Assessment and categorization of TLE orbit errors for the US SSN catalogue. AMOS Conference.
    8. Kessler, D. J. & Cour-Palais, B. G. (1978). Collision frequency of artificial satellites: the creation of a debris belt. JGR, 83(A6).
    9. ESA Space Environment Report and ISRO ISSAR 2024. isro.gov.in
